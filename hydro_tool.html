1 · Input data

How to use
1. Copy the Observed and Simulated columns from Excel and paste them in the yellow box (column 1 = Observed, column 2 = Simulated). A header row is skipped automatically.
2. Click Calculate.
3. Time steps with missing observed data (blank, text, -999 or negative) are excluded; the simulated series is still drawn on the hydrograph.
Separators accepted: tab, comma, semicolon or space.

MetricFormulaIdeal

PBIAS (%)100 × Σ(S−O) / ΣO (or Σ(O−S)/ΣO with Moriasi option)0

Ratings for NSE and PBIAS follow Moriasi et al. (2007) guidance for monthly streamflow and are indicative only. O = observed, S = simulated, n = number of valid pairs. Sample standard deviation (n−1) is used for KGE.
